How it works

Three steps, then just keep ticking.

  1. 1

    Choose your subjects

    Pick from 32 Year 12 subjects, plus maths competitions. Each one lists the HSC papers and the trial papers and assessment tasks that THSC Online has, grouped by school or by year.

  2. 2

    Tick papers as you do them

    Tap a paper to mark it done. Add your mark if you like. Each subject shows how far through you are, and the Completed list keeps the dates.

  3. 3

    Set a traffic light

    Tap the lights on a subject to say how you feel about it. The colour is your call; the tracker only shows a faint hint from your progress.

    Red: needs workAmber: getting thereGreen: confident
