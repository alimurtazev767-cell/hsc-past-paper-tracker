Free, with no account needed. Your ticks save in this browser.

Three stops, then just keep ticking.

  1. 1

    Choose your subjects

    Pick from 32 Year 12 subjects, plus maths competitions. Each one lists the HSC papers and the trial papers and assessment tasks that THSC Online has, grouped by school or by year.

  2. 2

    Tick papers as you do them

    Tick papers as practice and add your mark if you like. Each subject runs on a line through AT1, AT2, AT3, Trials and the HSC, so you always see your next stop.

  3. 3

    Set a traffic light

    Use the lights on a subject to say how you feel about it. The colour is your call; the tracker never picks it for you.

    Red: needs workAmber: getting thereGreen: confident

Built for NSW Year 12.

  • HSC papersFrom THSC Online up to 2018 and NESA after that.
  • Trials and assessmentsTrial papers, plus half yearlies and AT1 to AT4 where THSC has them.
  • Syllabus dot pointsGive each Year 12 dot point its own light in Maths, English, Chemistry, Physics and HMS.
  • Your own papersAdd a paper that isn't listed, like your school's own trial.
  • Saved for youTicks save in this browser, so come back on the same device.
  • Study timer and calendarTime your study by subject and see each week, with your assessment dates on the calendar.
