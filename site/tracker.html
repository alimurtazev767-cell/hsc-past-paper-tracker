Syllabus dot points are the Year 12 (HSC course) content of each subject's syllabus for the 2027 HSC, copied from NESA's NSW Curriculum website and NESA's syllabus documents; each subject links to its source. © NSW Education Standards Authority (NESA) for and on behalf of the Crown in right of the State of New South Wales. The official, up-to-date syllabuses are on NESA's websites; check there if anything here differs. This site isn't made or endorsed by NESA.

Trial papers and HSC papers up to 2018 are copied from THSC Online's Year 12 pages as listed on 2 October 2026. THSC's HSC lists stop at 2018, so newer HSC papers come from each subject's NESA exam paper page, checked the same day. Software Engineering and Enterprise Computing have only the 2025 NESA paper. Assessment tasks (half yearly and AT1 to AT4) come from THSC's assessment task pages, sorted the way THSC sorts them; THSC has these for 14 subjects.

Paper names come from THSC Online and NESA. This site only lists names so you can track your practice; get the papers themselves from those sites. Free and non-commercial, for students' private study.

Each subject's progress counts papers from the year you pick in that subject (defaults: Maths Advanced and the Extensions from 2020, everything else from 2019). Every year THSC lists is shown, as on THSC; untick "Show papers before" to see only recent years. Older papers you tick still count in the totals.
